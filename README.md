**MXX** is a Vanilla+ Fabric mod that improves how you earn, spend, and preserve experience in Minecraft, with configurable progression and useful enchanting improvements.

## Features

<details>
<summary>Experience Progression & Preservation</summary>

- **Constant XP curve:** Set a fixed XP requirement per level (30 XP by default).
- **Keep XP on death:** Retain a configurable percentage of your experience (100% by default).
- **XP orb attraction:** Increase the distance at which experience orbs follow players.

</details>

<details>
<summary>More Ways to Earn XP</summary>

- Earn XP from harvesting mature crops, breaking fully grown amethyst clusters, and shearing sheep.
- **Fortune, Looting, and Luck of the Sea** increase their respective XP rewards by **20% per enchantment level**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Bottles & Wisdom</summary>

- Brew Bottles o' Enchanting and optionally give them a fixed XP reward.
- Brew **Potions of Wisdom** to temporarily increase XP gained, with extended and stronger variants.
- Craft a **Catalyzed Bottle o' Enchanting** to store XP one level at a time and recover it by drinking.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Anvils, Grindstones & Mending</summary>

- Remove the anvil's *Too Expensive!* limit and prior-work penalty.
- Recover deterministic, configurable XP from Grindstones; optionally remove curses at a reduced XP reward.
- **Smart Mending:** Prioritize the equipped item with the highest percentage of missing durability.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Additional Vanilla+ Improvements</summary>

- Craft renewable Echo Shards.
- Collect Budding Amethyst with Silk Touch.
- Replace the master-level Cleric's Bottle o' Enchanting trade with Echo Shards.

</details>

## Requirements

- **Minecraft Java Edition 26.1.2** and **Fabric Loader**
- **Fabric API** and **fzzy_config**
- **Mod Menu** (optional, for in-game configuration)

MXX is required on both the client and server for multiplayer.

## Configuration

Most gameplay changes can be enabled, disabled, or adjusted through **fzzy_config**. Configuration settings and descriptions are available in English and Spanish.
